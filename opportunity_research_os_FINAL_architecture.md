# Opportunity Research OS
## Build Architecture for Discovering High-Quality Recurring SaaS Opportunities

> **Purpose:** Build an internal research operating system that continuously searches the economy for inefficient, recurring, expensive human workflows and converts them into evidence-backed SaaS opportunities.
>
> The system should not brainstorm random SaaS ideas. It should discover problems from real-world evidence, analyze markets and competitors, challenge its own conclusions, validate demand, and learn from actual outcomes.

---

# 1. Core Objective

We want to discover SaaS opportunities that have:

- Real, recurring pain
- Clear monthly-retainer potential
- Strong willingness to pay
- Reachable B2B buyers
- A workflow that can be substantially automated with AI
- Weaknesses in existing competition
- Good cold-email distribution
- Reasonable build complexity
- Fast time-to-value
- Low enough switching friction
- Strong retention potential
- Expansion potential
- Defensibility beyond “just an AI wrapper”

The ideal system moves from:

**Internet evidence → workflow understanding → market analysis → economic analysis → competition analysis → adversarial validation → customer testing → MVP recommendation**

---

# 2. Foundational Principle

Do not search for SaaS ideas directly.

Search for:

- Repetitive work
- Manual processes
- Expensive mistakes
- Unbilled revenue
- Compliance obligations
- Reconciliation pain
- Spreadsheet workflows
- Copy/paste workflows
- Internal scripts
- Human-heavy coordination
- Repetitive communication
- Work done by VAs, agencies, consultants, clerks, analysts, coordinators
- Problems that regenerate every week or month
- Workflows where current software is disliked, expensive, fragmented, or incomplete

The discovery direction is:

**Pain → Workflow → Economics → Existing Solutions → Gap → SaaS Opportunity**

Not:

**Idea → Try to prove it matters**

# 7. Specialist Analysis Agents

## 7.1 Pain Intelligence Agent

Goal:

Determine whether the complaint represents real business pain.

Extract:

- What exactly happens
- Who suffers
- How often
- Why it matters
- What happens if ignored
- Whether it is emotional annoyance or economic pain

Reject weak complaints such as:

> “This UI is ugly.”

Prefer:

> “Our controller spends six hours every Monday exporting three systems and reconciling them manually.”

---

- Excel

- Consultants

- Browser copy/paste
- Internal scripts
- Zapier/Make hacks

# 8. Shadow Workflow Agent

One of the highest-value components.

Detect unofficial workflows that happen outside formal software.

Example:

```text
PDF arrives by email
→ employee reads it
→ enters values into SAP
→ updates Excel
→ messages warehouse on WhatsApp
→ files PDF in Drive
```

This is effectively a SaaS specification hiding inside daily work.

The system should specifically search for combinations of:

- Email
- spreadsheets
- PDFs
- messaging
- browser tabs
- manual copying
- repetitive data entry

# 14. Money Agent

Estimate the economics of the pain.

Model:

```text
Labor cost
+ Revenue leakage
+ Error cost
+ Risk cost
+ Opportunity cost
+ Existing software/service spend
= Total economic pain
```

Then estimate realistic pricing.

Example:

```text
2 employees × 40 hours/month × $35/hour = $2,800
Revenue leakage = $1,500/month
Existing tool = $300/month

Total economic pain ≈ $4,600/month
Potential SaaS price = $500–$1,500/month
```

---

- Budget owner

A painful problem is less valuable if nobody has authority or budget to buy.

# 18. AI Advantage Agent

# 19. AI Obsolescence Agent

Prefer opportunities requiring:

- proprietary integrations
- operational workflow ownership
- historical data
- system actions
- domain-specific rules
- cross-system orchestration
- proprietary benchmarks
- specialized datasets

# 24. Unserved ICP Detector

A category may exist but serve the wrong segment.

Look for:

- Enterprise only
- SMB only
- US only
- Europe only
- Shopify Plus only
- healthcare only
- large fleets only
- companies above a certain order volume

Potential wedge:

> Same proven workflow, underserved segment.

---

# 25. Automation Threshold Detector

Pain often appears only after a company reaches a certain size.

Example:

```text
<50 orders/day → manual is fine
50–500 orders/day → painful
500+ → enterprise software
```

The underserved middle can be the opportunity.

---

# 26. Cross-Industry Pattern Transfer

Find workflows that are solved well in one industry but poorly in another.

Example:

```text
Medical billing reconciliation
→ Freight billing reconciliation
→ Construction billing reconciliation
→ Insurance reconciliation
```

This lets us borrow validated SaaS models rather than inventing everything from zero.

# 27. Bundling / Unbundling Engine

Ask:

### Unbundling
Can one painful feature be extracted from a bloated expensive suite?

### Bundling
Are customers stitching together five small tools that could become one coherent workflow product?

Both patterns can create strong SaaS businesses.

---

# 28. Integration-Gap Mapper

Map software ecosystems.

Example:

```text
System A ↔ System B
```

Look for repeated complaints about:

- no integration
- poor sync
- CSV exports
- duplicate entry
- delayed synchronization
- manual reconciliation

Integration products can be very sticky if tied to recurring operations.

# 29. Trigger-Event Agent

Identify moments when buyers suddenly care.

Examples:

- audit approaching
- new regulation
- employee quits
- volume spike
- fraud event
- chargeback spike
- company reaches 50 employees
- new warehouse
- new funding round
- rapid hiring
- switching ERP

---

# 30. Distribution Agent

Score how easy it is for us to sell.

Questions:

- Can we identify companies?
- Can we identify decision makers?
- Can emails be found?
- Are there 5k, 20k, or 100k reachable accounts?
- Can the value proposition fit in two sentences?
- Can ROI be demonstrated?
- Can the buyer approve without long procurement?

Strong cold-email product example:

> “We found $7,300 in unreconciled freight payments.”

Weak example:

> “Improve culture with AI.”

---

# 31. Sales-Cycle Agent

Estimate:

- Self-serve
- 1-call sale
- 2–4 week sale
- 3-month procurement
- 9-month enterprise sale

We should favor products where reasonable ACV can be sold without enormous procurement friction.

- security review

- insurance requirements
- data processing agreements

# 42. Pain Trajectory Engine

Classify pain as:

### Growing
Problem gets worse every year.

### Stable
Always exists.

### Shrinking
Likely to be solved by platforms or automation anyway.

Prefer growing pain.

---

# 43. Market Saturation Trajectory

Track not just competitors today but:

- number of startups entering
- funding velocity
- launch frequency
- new features
- M&A
- incumbent expansion

Five competitors in a quiet niche may be better than two competitors in a category suddenly receiving massive VC attention.

---

# 44. TAM Realism Layer

Do not use generic market-size numbers.

Calculate reachable market:

```text
Number of exact-fit companies
× realistic conversion rate
× realistic monthly price
```

Example:

```text
18,000 reachable companies
× 5% eventual penetration
× $1,000 MRR
= $900k MRR theoretical reachable segment
```

# 48. Contrarian Agent

Its only job is to destroy ideas.

Questions:

- Why has nobody solved this?
- Is this actually painful?
- Is Reddit exaggerating?
- Is there already a free solution?
- Will companies change behavior?
- Can current software add this?
- Is integration too difficult?
- Is buyer urgency low?
- Will AI commoditize this?
- Is the market too small?
- Are customers unwilling to switch?

No idea should advance without surviving this agent.

---

# 49. Contradiction Engine

Search specifically for evidence against our thesis.

If we believe:

> “Accountants hate workflow X”

search for:

- accountants saying X is fine
- recent product updates solving X
- cheap alternatives
- automation already built
- lack of budget
- low urgency
- failed startups in the category

This prevents confirmation bias.

---

# 50. False-Positive Killer

Many complaints are commercially worthless.

Require evidence that pain is:

- frequent
- expensive
- recurring
- owned by someone with budget
- measurable
- operationally important

---

# 51. Sales Objection Simulator

Generate realistic objections.

Examples:

- “QuickBooks already does this.”
- “We don't trust AI.”
- “Our employee already handles it.”
- “Migration isn't worth it.”
- “Security won't approve this.”
- “We only have this problem twice a year.”

Then determine whether positioning/product design can overcome them.

---

# 52. Confidence Calibration

Every score should have confidence.

Example:

```text
Opportunity score: 87/100
Confidence: 41%
```

Do not pretend weak evidence is certainty.

---

# 53. Evidence Diversity Rule

No idea advances based on one source type.

Strong opportunity should have at least three independent signal categories, such as:

```text
Reddit complaints
+ job postings
+ G2 reviews
+ agency spend
+ customer interviews
```

Correlated evidence should be down-weighted.

---

# 54. Opportunity Genealogy

Every opportunity should display exactly why it exists.

Example:


# 64. Learning Brain

Example:

```text

Actual: 1.4%

Predicted price: $1,000/month

```

Then adjust future weights.

Over time, the system learns what our model consistently overestimates.

# 97. Implemented Signal Processing Contract

Every collected `RAW_DOCUMENT` is transformed through one idempotent pipeline:

```text
RAW DOCUMENT
        -> deterministic text normalization + language detection
        -> global exact hash deduplication
        -> near-duplicate SimHash comparison
        -> structured pain and workflow extraction
        -> evidence-quote verification
        -> vector embedding
        -> semantic deduplication
        -> durable SIGNAL + SIGNAL_EXTRACTION
```

The extraction contract includes:

- Pain status, probability, type, and confidence
- Industry, subindustry, company type, and company size
- User role, buyer role, trigger, task, and current workflow
- Tools, software, inputs, outputs, and manual steps
- Frequency, recurrence, time spent, workaround, and current spend
- Failure, revenue, risk, and compliance consequences
- Urgency, desired outcome, summary, and source-grounded evidence

AI-derived values must retain:

```text
prompt name and semantic version
requested model and actual routed model
provider
input hash
structured output
token usage
cost
latency
confidence
verified evidence spans
timestamp
```

Model calls use strict JSON Schema and an ordered fallback route. Embeddings use a fixed 1,536-dimension contract so pgvector can maintain a cosine HNSW index. Exact and near-duplicate checks happen before model calls to avoid unnecessary cost; semantic comparison happens after embedding.

If model credentials are unavailable, deterministic processing remains valid and the signal enters `awaiting_model`. It is later resumed through the same idempotent pipeline instead of being discarded or partially invented.

# 98. Implemented Evidence Intelligence Contract

Processed canonical signals feed a deterministic graph-and-cluster layer:

```text
SIGNAL + EXTRACTION + EMBEDDING
        -> normalized entities
        -> evidence-backed signal/entity links
        -> supported entity relationships
        -> pain, workflow, and change descriptors
        -> exact fingerprint assignment
        -> anchored semantic assignment
        -> cluster evidence and velocity metrics
```

The knowledge graph represents:

- Industries and subindustries
- Named companies, company types, and company sizes
- Users and budget owners
- Triggers, tasks, pain types, and recurrence
- Tools, current software, inputs, and outputs
- Workflow and manual steps
- Workarounds and desired outcomes

Graph relationships retain support counts and the signal IDs that created them. A repeated relationship therefore becomes stronger through independent evidence instead of being treated as an unsupported model assertion.

Every qualifying signal may participate in three complementary cluster families:

```text
PAIN CLUSTER      recurring costly or risky problem
WORKFLOW CLUSTER  repeated operational process
CHANGE CLUSTER    external event capable of creating new work
```

Assignment follows these safeguards:

1. Stable ontology fingerprints are checked first.
2. Semantic similarity is considered only within the same cluster family.
3. Normal semantic matches require a shared industry, role, trigger, task, or pain anchor.
4. Unanchored matches require a stricter similarity threshold.
5. Duplicate signals never inflate cluster strength.
6. Cluster processing is idempotent, retryable, and records stale or failed jobs.

Each cluster stores:

```text
signal_count
independent_source_count
evidence_category_count
corroboration_score
recurrence_score
first_seen_at
last_seen_at
velocity_30d
centroid
supporting entities
supporting signal memberships
```

Corroboration is bounded and increases through source diversity, evidence-category diversity, repeated signals, and recurrence. It is evidence strength—not an opportunity score. Only the next opportunity-hypothesis layer may translate sufficiently corroborated clusters into possible businesses.

```text
RAW
-> CLUSTERED
-> SCREENED
-> RESEARCHING
-> THESIS_READY
-> VALIDATING
-> PILOT / PROTOTYPE
-> BUILD
```

Any appropriate stage may move to `WATCHLIST` or `KILLED`. Transitions are validated, require a reason, and are appended to an immutable audit history.

Opportunity genealogy retains:

- The origin cluster
- Supporting and contextual clusters
- Shared graph entities
- Supporting signal IDs
- Contradicting signal IDs
- Source categories
- Gate checks
- Link confidence
- First and most recent observation timestamps

Related non-pain evidence is searched automatically for pain-led hypotheses and stored as contradiction. Contradictions reduce confidence but do not silently erase a hypothesis. Evidence and cluster links that cease to qualify become inactive instead of being deleted, preserving the complete research history.

This is still a hypothesis layer, not final opportunity scoring. Competition, spend verification, market size, distribution, buildability, defensibility, and adversarial research remain mandatory before an opportunity can become a complete thesis.

# 100. Implemented Autonomous Specialist Research Contract

Every evidence-gated hypothesis now receives one versioned research campaign containing five
required specialist lanes:

```text
COMPETITION   -> direct, adjacent, service, free, and internal alternatives
ECONOMICS     -> labor cost, consequence cost, existing spend, buyer, and budget
MARKET        -> exact-fit reachable accounts and bottom-up demand proxies
DISTRIBUTION  -> directories, communities, events, triggers, and sales friction
CONTRADICTION -> incumbent coverage, free displacement, weak urgency, switching, and failure risk
```

The planner derives concrete search queries from the hypothesis ICP, workflow, pain, user, buyer,
current software, industry, and trigger. Each lane has bounded document limits and its own
collection task, targets, status, retry count, coverage, timing, cost, and traceable run IDs.

Research collection uses the same Apify run and webhook infrastructure as the signal collectors.
The research source is created in `review_required` state. Missing credentials, an absent Actor, or
unapproved access never crashes or bypasses policy; the campaign waits in
`awaiting_configuration` and the scheduler resumes it when configuration becomes valid.

Each specialist is constrained by a versioned strict JSON schema. It receives only the opportunity
context and collected research packet. Claimed evidence is retained only when its URL exists in the
packet and its quote can be found in the corresponding stored document. The system records the
requested and actual model, input fingerprint, prompt version, usage, cost, latency, output, and
failure details for every analysis run.

Research produces:

- Versioned lane findings with stance, confidence, coverage, source URLs, and document IDs
- Normalized, updatable competitor profiles linked to the competition finding
- Bottom-up economics, market, and distribution structures
- A contradiction verdict of `survives`, `watchlist`, or `kill`
- Campaign-level lane coverage, cost, completion state, and recommended disposition

The research gate passes only when all five lanes complete, average coverage is at least `0.60`,
every lane has at least `0.40` coverage, and contradiction does not return `kill`. Passing this gate
does not yet promote the hypothesis to `THESIS_READY`; the next scoring and red-team layer will
combine these results with feasibility, defensibility, risk, and confidence penalties before making
that transition.

# 101. Implemented Opportunity Decision and Red-Team Contract

Completed research campaigns now enter a versioned investment-decision pipeline. The score is an
auditable 100-point model using the architecture's committed weights:

```text
Pain severity                12%
Recurring frequency          10%
Existing spend                9%
Willingness to pay            8%
Competition weakness         10%
Distribution quality         10%
Retention                     8%
Buildability                  7%
Time-to-value                 6%
AI advantage                  5%
Defensibility                 5%
Market timing                 4%
Reachable TAM                 3%
Expansion                     3%
```

Pain, recurrence, spend, willingness-to-pay, competition, distribution, timing, and reachable TAM
are calculated deterministically from the hypothesis and specialist research. Retention,
buildability, time-to-value, AI advantage, defensibility, and expansion use a strict expert and
contrarian schema. The model must return exactly those six dimensions and cannot change weights,
thresholds, penalties, or lifecycle behavior.

Each dimension persists:

```text
raw score
weight
weighted contribution
confidence
rationale
calculation inputs and assumptions
verified evidence URLs or supporting signal IDs
```

Confidence is separate from opportunity quality. Unsupported expert claims receive low confidence;
research coverage gaps, low-confidence dimensions, verified high risks, and watchlist verdicts are
visible penalties rather than hidden adjustments.

The red-team analysis explicitly records data and integration requirements, AI advantage,
defensibility, expansion path, time-to-value, realistic objections, risks, mitigations, kill
criteria, and answers to the ten central-brain investment questions. Every factual citation is
checked against the stored source document. Unsupported AI `kill` opinions cannot kill a thesis;
they force watchlist treatment. Only source-supported hard kills or the deterministic low-score
threshold can produce `KILLED`.

The deterministic disposition engine applies:

```text
hard kill or adjusted score below 40
    -> KILLED

adjusted score at least 70
+ confidence at least 0.65
+ research gate passed
+ red-team verdict advance
    -> THESIS_READY

everything else
    -> WATCHLIST
```

Thresholds are configuration values and all lifecycle changes enter the immutable transition
history. Failed expert runs retry at most three times automatically. Without an OpenRouter key,
decisions remain safely in `awaiting_analysis` and resume when configuration becomes available.

# 102. Implemented Decision Control Center and Pipeline Reliability Contract

The visual control center is now the operating surface for the complete evidence-to-decision
pipeline. Its primary object is a ranked evidence ledger, not a collection of disconnected metric
cards. Every row combines opportunity identity, track, proof volume, score or hypothesis
confidence, and current disposition. Selecting a row opens the complete auditable dossier without
losing portfolio context.

The interface exposes:

```text
portfolio rank and filters
collection-to-decision throughput
selected thesis and research-lane state
14-dimension score decomposition
dimension confidence and penalties
supporting and contradicting evidence
specialist research findings
reachable market and economics
competitor map
risk and mitigation register
immutable lifecycle history
operational attention queue
source access state
human research, score, watchlist, and kill controls
```

The UI reads the canonical APIs and does not maintain independent business logic. It renders
truthful offline and empty states, lazy-loads deeper dossiers, supports keyboard focus and reduced
motion, and collapses from the desktop evidence ledger into a usable mobile operating surface.
Browser mutations are restricted by configured CORS origins.

Before implementing this surface, Steps 1–7 were re-audited. The following production gaps were
closed with forward-only migration `0008` and worker changes:

- The custom public-web Actor now accepts bounded `searchQueries`, uses the configured Apify search
  Actor for URL discovery, respects target `robots.txt`, extracts full content, and emits the
  existing stable dataset contract.
- The same Actor can serve both approved source collection and autonomous specialist research.
- Running Apify jobs are polled and ingested when a webhook is absent or delayed.
- Transient Apify network failures remain retryable instead of incorrectly becoming terminal.
- Scheduled sources cannot launch before Actor configuration and access approval.
- Signal processing, intelligence clustering, specialist research, and final decision retries are
  bounded to prevent runaway model spend.

The control center is observational and operational, not a new decision authority. Research gates,
score weights, hard kills, confidence thresholds, and lifecycle transitions remain enforced by the
backend contracts documented in Sections 97–101.

# 103. Implemented Real-World Validation and Experiment Registry Contract

Every completed `advance` decision now produces one versioned validation campaign containing four
precommitted experiment types:

```text
pain interview
cold outreach
pricing conversation
paid commitment
```

Each experiment stores its causal hypothesis, target audience, channel, positioning variable,
primary metric, guardrails, minimum sample, success threshold, and execution brief. The OS plans and
measures these experiments but does not own or send through external outreach infrastructure.

Outcome imports are idempotent and retain quantitative metrics, qualitative feedback, evidence URLs,
interpretation, source, and observation time. The deterministic evaluator distinguishes insufficient
samples from weak results. It uses pain confirmation, positive replies, pricing acceptance, calls,
LOIs, and paid commitments without allowing generic interest to substitute for commercial intent.

```text
insufficient precommitted sample -> PENDING
meaningful but incomplete proof  -> MIXED
pain + outreach + price proof    -> STRONG
deposit / paid pilot / pre-sale  -> STRONG + PILOT
sufficient weak buyer response   -> WEAK + WATCHLIST + FAILURE MEMORY
explicit disconfirmation         -> INVALIDATED + WATCHLIST + FAILURE MEMORY
```

Failure memory preserves the predicted thesis, actual buyer response, rejection category, and a
reusable calibration lesson. This is the first durable input for the later learning brain. The
Decision Control Center now includes a validation ledger with experiment state, sample progress,
predeclared success rules, and outcome-entry controls.

# 104. Implemented Learning Brain and Research Freshness Contract

The learning layer records immutable business outcomes against the decision that predicted them.
The event taxonomy covers positive outreach, demos, pricing acceptance, LOIs, paid pilots,
activation, retention, churn, and observed MRR. Event keys make imports idempotent; event properties
are restricted to non-PII operational metadata.

Calibration preserves every original prediction and computes prediction-to-outcome correlation only
from opportunities with complete scorecards and real validation or commercial outcomes. It will not
propose an active profile below the minimum sample. Proposed changes remain normalized, cannot move
any dimension by more than two percentage points per run, and require a named human reviewer.
Approval affects future decisions only; historical scores are never rewritten.

Research freshness is a parallel, versioned lifecycle:

```text
completed baseline research
    -> time/priority freshness assessment
    -> separately versioned refresh campaign
    -> compare structured findings by lane
    -> surface detected changes + requires_rescore
    -> human decides whether to score again
```

High-scoring and actively piloted opportunities receive shorter freshness windows. Refresh research
covers competitors, pricing, regulation, product launches, reviews, market sentiment, APIs, and
funding. The scheduler can use the custom Apify Actor only when its token, Actor ID, and approved
source are present. Refresh campaigns cannot enter the automatic decision-scoring queue, preventing
new web evidence from silently changing an investment decision.

The Learning tab exposes the outcome registry, freshness score and next check, refresh controls,
detected changes, calibration quality, recommended weight deltas, and explicit profile approval.

# 105. Implemented Portfolio Operations and Build Handoff Contract

This is the final core subsystem. The OS now closes the complete loop from external signal to an
approved, evidence-backed product build plan.

The portfolio monitor retains current and previous scores and ranks, calculates score, pain,
competition, and market-timing velocity, and classifies each opportunity as improving, stable,
deteriorating, saturating, or invalidated. Material events enter a deduplicated database alert
outbox. Telegram is an optional delivery adapter, not a parallel source of truth, and an absent bot
configuration never blocks the monitoring loop.

Customer-discovery conversations are first-class structured evidence. Interview records preserve
current workflow, tools, frequency, time and financial cost, current spend, complaints, desired
outcome, urgency, budget and price reactions, objections, selected quotes, follow-up, and explicit
evidence strength. Pattern aggregation distinguishes buyer evidence, specific spend, repeated tools,
and recurring complaints.

The Build-Spec Generator is locked behind a completed `strong` validation verdict. Each version is
bound to the exact decision and validation campaign that justified it and carries a source manifest.
It produces:

```text
product definition and differentiator
MUST HAVE / SHOULD HAVE / LATER / DO NOT BUILD scope
user stories and end-to-end product workflow
technical requirements, initial data model, and integration plan
AI versus deterministic responsibility boundary
onboarding and time-to-first-value plan
pricing hypothesis, demo script, and outreach positioning
validation-to-build checklist
30 / 60 / 90-day execution plan
```

Build specifications are human-approved and exportable as Markdown. The final control-center stage
uses a decision runway—portfolio rank to market momentum to interview proof to build readiness—so
the operator can see why a thesis is or is not ready for engineering.

With Sections 96–105 implemented, the foundational architecture is frozen. Further additions are
data sources, experiments, adapters, or optimizations unless real usage demonstrates a structural
failure.
